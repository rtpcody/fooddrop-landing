Built for creators

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

FoodDrop gives you the tools to run a professional food business without the complexity of tools built for restaurants.

📊

Creator Dashboard

Manage drops, track orders in real time, and see your revenue at a glance — all in one clean view.

🧑‍🍳

Auto Prep List

When your drop closes, FoodDrop calculates exactly how much of each item to prepare. No spreadsheet needed.

👥

Customer CRM

Every order builds your customer list automatically. Know your regulars, track their history, and build the kind of loyalty that keeps people coming back drop after drop.

🔔

Order Management

Track the status of every order — confirmed, paid, picked up. Your operation stays organized without any effort.

🛍️

Customer Storefront

Your customers get a clean, mobile-friendly page to browse your drop and place their order. No app download required.

✉️

Reach Your People

Message your customers directly from the dashboard — announce new drops, thank your regulars, or re-engage anyone who hasn't ordered in a while.
